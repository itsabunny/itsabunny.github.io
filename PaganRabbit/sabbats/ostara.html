March 20–21 • Spring Equinox

Ostara in the PaganRabbit Tradition

The Leaping Spring – PaganRabbit's Great Festival

Symbolism

Perfect balance between day and night. Light has triumphed over darkness, and from this moment forward, the days will be longer than the nights. The earth bursts with new life, fertility, and unstoppable growth. This is the true beginning of spring.

The Rabbit's Wisdom

This is the rabbit's moment of pure joy! The meadow is green, flowers are blooming, and the air is warm enough to play. Rabbits perform their famous "binkies" – those ecstatic leaps and twists in mid-air that express nothing but the sheer delight of being alive. Ostara is the heart of PaganRabbit spirituality, named for the goddess Ēostre who was accompanied by a hare.

Step 1: Gather Your Items

Simple Household Inventory

Celebrate the explosion of life and color:

Fresh flowers
Daffodils, tulips, crocuses, or any spring blooms you can find. Even a handful of dandelions from the lawn is perfect!
Eggs or stones
Hard-boiled eggs you can decorate, or smooth stones painted in spring colors. Symbols of potential and new life.
Green candles
Green for the vibrant meadow, or pastel colors (yellow, pink, lavender) for spring blossoms.
Seeds to plant
Wildflower seeds, herb seeds, or vegetable seeds – something you'll plant outside or in a pot.
Fresh spring greens
Lettuce, young spinach, pea shoots, or fresh herbs for your feast.

Step 2: Build Your Ostara Altar

Create a meadow in miniature

Cover your altar with a green cloth or fresh grass clippings. This is the vibrant meadow the rabbit dances upon.

Arrange the flowers

Place your fresh flowers in a vase or scattered across the altar. Let it be colorful and abundant – spring holds nothing back!

Decorate your eggs or stones

Use natural dyes, paint, or markers to create beautiful patterns. Traditional symbols include spirals (growth), suns (returning light), and flowers. Place them around your candles.

Step 3: The Ritual

Step by Step

Part 1: Burst from the Burrow

The Great Emergence

Stand before your altar. Imagine you've been underground all winter. Now, the earth above is warm and green and calling you.

Take a deep breath and rise onto your toes (or simply straighten up tall). Feel yourself emerging into the sunlight.

Say with energy:

"I leap from the darkness into the light!
Winter is over – spring has come!
The meadow is green and I am alive!"

Part 2: Light the Candles of Balance

Light your candles. Today, light and dark are equal, but from tomorrow forward, light will lead.

As the flames catch, say:

"Day and night stand equal.
From this moment, light grows stronger.
I welcome the rising sun and the greening earth."

Part 3: The Dance of Joy (The Binky!)

Move your body!

If you are able, and if you feel moved to do so: jump! Leap in the air, twist, spin – even if it's just a little hop or a seated arm-raise. This is the rabbit's binky – the physical expression of pure, uncomplicated joy.

You can do this indoors or go outside. Let your body celebrate being alive.

If jumping isn't accessible to you, dance with your hands, sway in your seat, or simply smile widely and feel the energy rise in your chest.

As you move, think or shout:

"I am here! I am alive! Spring is HERE!"

Part 4: Plant Your Seeds

Give back to the earth

Take your seeds outside if you can. Find a patch of earth, a pot, or a community garden plot. Plant them with intention.

As you place each seed or scatter your wildflower mix, think about what you're offering back to the world – beauty, food, habitat for bees and butterflies.

Speak your blessing:

"I plant these seeds as a gift to the earth.
May they grow green and strong.
May they nourish and delight all who encounter them."

Part 5: The Spring Feast

Eat the green earth

Prepare a completely green meal: a fresh salad with lettuce, cucumber, herbs, snap peas, and avocado. Dress it with lemon and olive oil. Add fresh bread if you like.

Eat it outside if possible, or by a sunny window. Taste the vibrant life force in every bite.

Part 6: Closing – The Promise of Growth

Stand before your altar one last time.

"Spring has arrived in full force.
I celebrate the balance, the growth, and the wild joy of life.
From this day forward, I walk in the light."

Let your candles burn down, and keep your flowers on display until they fade. Every time you see them, remember: you are alive, and that is worth celebrating.

Step 4: Carrying Spring Forward

Late March and Beyond

Take a "rabbit eyes" nature walk

Go outside and look at the world from low to the ground. Notice the tiny flowers, the new grass, the busy insects. See the world as a rabbit sees it.

Wear bright colors

Let go of winter's dark, heavy clothes. Bring out the greens, yellows, and pastels. Let your wardrobe reflect the season.

Share the joy

Give someone flowers. Share your fresh greens. Tell a friend about something that delights you. Spring is not meant to be hoarded – it overflows and spreads.
