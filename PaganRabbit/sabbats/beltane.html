May 1

Beltane in the PaganRabbit Tradition

The Green Meadow & Abundance

Symbolism

The height of spring. Nature is in full bloom, life force pulses through everything, and the world is utterly green and lush. This is a festival of passion, creativity, fertility (in all its forms), and the sheer pleasure of being alive in a world overflowing with beauty.

The Rabbit's Wisdom

The meadow is now a paradise. Clover blooms everywhere, dandelions carpet the ground like little golden suns, and the grass is tender and sweet. Rabbits move through this abundance with playful energy, enjoying the feast that the earth provides. This is the season of coupling, of play, and of celebrating life in its most vibrant expression.

Step 1: Gather Your Items

Simple Household Inventory

Embrace the fullness of life:

Flowers & greenery
As many as you can gather! Daisies, clover, buttercups, dandelions, or any wildflowers. Plus long grasses, ivy, or leafy branches.
Colorful ribbons
Green, pink, yellow, white – bright, cheerful ribbons for weaving and decorating.
Green & red candles
Green for the meadow, red for passion and life force.
Fresh green foods
Lettuce, herbs (especially mint and basil), snap peas, green beans, cucumbers, and anything else vibrant and crisp.
Maple syrup or sweet treats
Maple syrup, agave nectar, fresh berries, or sweet vegan cakes – symbols of the sweetness of life.

Step 2: Build Your Beltane Altar

Create an explosion of green

Cover your altar completely with fresh greenery and flowers. Don't hold back – Beltane is about abundance, not minimalism!

Weave a flower crown or garland

Use your flowers and ribbons to make a crown for yourself or a garland for your altar. Weaving is meditative and connects you to the interlacing of life.

Place your candles among the blooms

Nestle the green and red candles into your altar greenery. The flames will dance among the petals.

Step 3: The Ritual

Step by Step

Part 1: Step into the Meadow

Arriving in Fullness

Stand before your altar. If possible, go outside and do this ritual in an actual meadow or park. If not, your flower-covered altar is your meadow.

Close your eyes and feel the green world around you. Imagine the hum of bees, the scent of flowers, the soft grass under your feet.

Say:

"I stand in the green heart of the world.
Life overflows around me and within me.
I am part of this great blooming."

Part 2: Light the Fires of Life

Light your green candle first, then your red candle.

As you light the green:

"The meadow is alive and abundant. I honor the green growing world."

As you light the red:

"Passion and vitality flow through all living things. I honor the life force that connects us all."

Part 3: The Wish Knot

Weaving your desire

Take a long blade of grass or a flexible green stem. As you slowly tie it into a loose knot, think of something you wish to bloom in your life – a project, a relationship, a quality, a dream.

The knot holds your intention. When you release it, you're releasing that wish into the fertile world to grow in its own way and time.

As you tie the knot, whisper:

"I tie this wish into the green world. May it grow as the flowers grow."

Place the knotted grass on your altar, or tuck it into the earth outside.

Part 4: The Green Feast

Eating abundance

Prepare a feast of entirely green foods. Make a huge salad, steam green beans, slice cucumbers, chop fresh herbs over everything. Drizzle with good olive oil and a squeeze of lemon.

Add something sweet – fresh strawberries, a drizzle of maple syrup on fruit, or a piece of vegan cake.

Eat with full presence. Taste the green earth. Feel its nourishment.

Before you eat, give thanks:

"Thank you to the generous earth.
Thank you to the sun and rain.
Thank you for this abundance that I share in."

Part 5: Closing – Carry the Green Forward

Put on your flower crown if you made one. Wear it for the rest of the day, even if just around the house.

"I am crowned with flowers.
I am alive in the fullness of spring.
May I carry this green joy with me through all my days."

Let your candles burn down. Keep your flowers as long as they last, and when they fade, compost them with gratitude.

Step 4: Living in the Green

May and Beyond

Spend time in green spaces

Parks, gardens, meadows, forests – go where things grow. Sit in the grass. Lie on your back and look up through the leaves. Let the green world soak into you.

Dance, create, play

Beltane energy is creative and expressive. Make art, sing, dance, write, build something. Let the life force move through you into form.

Notice what blooms in you

Pay attention to what grows in your life over the coming weeks. What are you drawn to? What feels alive? That's your Beltane wish flowering.
