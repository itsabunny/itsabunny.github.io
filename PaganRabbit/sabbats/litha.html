June 20–22 • Summer Solstice

Litha in the PaganRabbit Tradition

The Sunbath & Midday Peace

Symbolism

The sun reaches its zenith. This is the longest day of the year, the peak of solar power. From this moment, the days will slowly shorten again, but for now – for this one perfect day – light reigns supreme and the world basks in warmth and abundance.

The Rabbit's Wisdom

On a warm summer afternoon, the rabbit finds a perfect spot – perhaps in the dappled shade beneath a bush, or stretched out in a gentle sunbeam. It performs the ultimate "flop," lying completely flat and still, utterly relaxed. This is total security, perfect contentment, and the wisdom of knowing when to simply rest and be. Litha celebrates this moment of peace and fulfillment.

Step 1: Gather Your Items

Simple Household Inventory

Celebrate the sun at its height:

Gold/yellow candles
Candles in sun colors – gold, yellow, orange. These represent the solar fire at its peak.
Summer flowers
Sunflowers, daisies, chamomile, St. John's wort, or any bright blooms.
Fresh berries
Strawberries, raspberries, blueberries – summer's sweet harvest.
Maple syrup or agave
Golden, sweet, and solar – plant-based sweetness that captures summer's warmth.
Summer herbs
Lavender, mint, chamomile, lemon balm – herbs at their most potent, ready to be dried.
Something comfortable
A blanket, cushion, or mat for lying outside in the sun.

Step 2: Build Your Litha Altar

Create a sun shrine

Use a golden or yellow cloth. Arrange your candles in a circle or sunburst pattern.

Add brightness and abundance

Place sunflowers at the center (or draw/print a sun symbol). Surround with berries, maple syrup, and your summer herbs.

Make it golden

If you have any gold-colored objects (ribbons, coins, bells), add them. The altar should shimmer and glow.

Step 3: The Ritual

Step by Step – Best done outdoors at midday or sunset

Part 1: Greet the Sun at Its Height

Solar Salutation

Go outside at noon (or as close to it as you can). Stand in the sunlight. Close your eyes and turn your face directly toward the sun. Feel the warmth on your skin.

Take a deep breath and acknowledge: this is the moment of maximum light. The sun will never be higher this year than it is right now.

Say aloud:

"Hail to the sun at the peak of its power!
You have reached your throne in the sky.
I celebrate your light, your warmth, your life-giving fire."

Part 2: Light the Solar Flames

Return to your altar (or bring your candles outside). Light each golden candle.

As the flames catch, say:

"The sun burns bright above.
These flames honor the light that sustains all life.
I am warmed, I am blessed, I am grateful."

Part 3: The Perfect Flop

Embodying Contentment

This is the heart of Litha in the PaganRabbit tradition.

Find a comfortable spot outside – in the sun or in pleasant shade. Lay out your blanket. Lie down completely. Let your whole body relax into the earth.

Let go of tension. Let go of striving. Let go of worry. Just be here, alive, warm, and safe.

Stay here for at least 10 minutes. Longer if you can. This is not lazy – this is sacred rest. This is trusting that you are held.

As you lie there, think or whisper:

"I am safe. I am peaceful. I am exactly where I need to be."

Part 4: The Summer Feast

Tasting the Sun

Eat your berries and taste the sunshine stored in their sweetness. Drizzle maple syrup or agave on bread or fruit. Drink cool water infused with mint or lemon balm.

Eat slowly, savoring every bite. Summer's abundance is fleeting – honor it fully.

Part 5: Harvest Your Herbs

Preserving the Sun

Litha is the traditional time to harvest herbs for drying. They're at their peak potency now, filled with solar energy.

If you have herbs growing (even a pot of basil or mint on the windowsill), snip some. Hang them to dry in bundles, or lay them on a screen.

These dried herbs will carry summer's warmth into the winter months.

Part 6: Closing – Honoring the Turn

As the sun begins to set on this longest day, acknowledge the turning of the wheel.

"The sun has reached its peak and now begins its slow descent.
I do not mourn – this is the way of all things.
Light and dark, growth and rest, the wheel keeps turning.
I honor the fullness of this moment and release it with grace."

Let your candles burn down as the sun sets. Carry the feeling of the flop – that perfect peace – with you as a touchstone.

Step 4: Carrying the Summer Light

Late June and Beyond

Practice the daily flop

Even for just five minutes. Find a moment each day to completely relax, to trust, to rest. This is a spiritual practice.

Dry your herbs and make sun tea

Preserve the magic. Brew tea in the sunlight (sun tea method). Store your dried herbs in jars labeled "Summer 2026" so you remember their potency when winter comes.

Notice the light changing

From now on, the days will shorten. Don't dread it – simply witness it. The wheel turns. There is wisdom in every season.
